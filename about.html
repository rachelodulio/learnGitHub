About Me

My interest in technology started from simple curiosity. Growing up, I enjoyed playing games like Y8 and Roblox, which made me wonder how these digital systems actually work behind the scenes. This curiosity eventually led me to pursue the field of Information Technology and explore the world of programming.

As a developer, I enjoy working on both the creative and technical sides of development. I particularly like designing user interfaces and building backend logic that makes applications functional and efficient. I have experience working with Java, HTML, CSS, and Python.

Some of the projects I'm most proud of include a Library Management System and an Inventory Management System with database integration. I approach projects with a detail-oriented, creative, and problem-solving mindset.

Personal Philosophy

Outside of coding, I believe that critical thinking and curiosity are essential for growth in technology.
